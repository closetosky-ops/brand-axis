02 — Content Direction

Never wonder what to post again.
Know what every piece of content is doing.

Monthly strategy and direction for businesses that need a stronger social presence without guessing, trend-chasing or creating content just to stay active.

Start Here →All Services
Who

Who this is for

For service businesses, product businesses and growing brands that know what they sell but struggle to turn that into a consistent, intentional Instagram presence. You may have someone filming, designing or posting already — but no clear direction behind it.

What

What is getting in the way

Random posting creates activity, not momentum. You may have good photos and videos but weak hooks, repetitive ideas, inconsistent offers, or no clear connection between what you post and what you want people to do next.

Why

Why it matters

Good content has a job. Some content attracts. Some explains. Some proves. Some sells. A monthly strategy makes sure those jobs work together instead of competing for attention.

What you receive

Clear thinking.
Useful direction.
No filler.

Every recommendation is built around your actual business, audience and goals.

  1. 01
    Monthly content direction

    A clear monthly theme and strategic focus based on your goals, offers and audience.

  2. 02
    Content pillars & message priorities

    We define what your brand should consistently talk about — and why those topics matter.

  3. 03
    Reel concepts, hooks & angles

    Specific concepts built around your business rather than generic lists of trending ideas.

  4. 04
    Campaign & offer direction

    We map content around launches, promotions, services, seasonal moments and conversion goals.

  5. 05
    Monthly content plan

    A practical plan showing what to create, what to post and the purpose behind each piece.

  6. 06
    Performance-informed adjustments

    We review what is resonating and refine the next direction instead of repeating the same plan every month.

How it works

A polished process from clarity to action.

01

Review

We look at the month ahead: goals, offers, events, launches, priorities and recent performance.

02

Position

We decide what people need to understand, remember or believe about your business this month.

03

Plan

We build the concepts, hooks, messages and content mix around that objective.

04

Create or hand off

You can execute the plan internally, with your own creator, or upgrade to Brand Axis Creative Production.

Questions

Before you book.

Everything you need to know before choosing Content Direction.

Do you post the content for me?

Content Direction focuses on the strategy and plan. If you need Brand Axis to create the assets too, Creative Production is the best fit.

Can I use my own photographer or videographer?

Absolutely. The strategy can be handed to your internal team or existing creative partners so everyone knows exactly what to produce.

Is this a monthly service?

Yes. Content Direction is designed to evolve with your business, offers and performance from month to month.

Do I get actual post ideas?

Yes. The plan includes specific concepts, hooks, angles and campaign ideas — not just broad content pillars.

Can this help with ad creative?

Yes. We can identify messages and concepts that are strong candidates for paid creative, while full ad account management is separate from this service.

What happens next

If you want Brand Axis to execute the strategy, Creative Production turns the monthly direction into polished Instagram-ready photo and video assets.
